Privacy Policy

Printwell — printer and scanner app for Android (Google Play) · Effective date: 10 November 2026

Printwell for Android (“the app”, package com.gokctech.com.print) is published on Google Play by gokchetech (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. Printwell works on your device and over your own network. We do not run any server that receives, stores or analyses your documents, photos, scans or usage. This policy explains what the app touches on your phone or tablet, and why. The iPhone and iPad version has its own policy.

At a glance

  • We collect no personal data through the app. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Documents, photos and scans go from your device straight to your printer over your own network. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • Text recognition and document scanning use Google’s on-device ML Kit, which may send anonymous diagnostic data to Google (section 3).
  • We sell no data.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal data through Printwell. In particular:

  • There are no user accounts. You never sign up, sign in or give us an email address to use the app.
  • Your documents, photos, scans, recognised text, print history and printer list are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs of ours or of third parties, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • Printwell does not access your location, contacts, microphone, SMS, call log or health data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we have none.

2. Permissions and what they are used for

  • Network access, Wi‑Fi state and multicast: to find printers and scanners on your Wi‑Fi (Bonjour/mDNS), to send print and scan jobs to them and to show your printer’s ink, paper and error status. Plain, unencrypted connections are allowed only to devices on your local network.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later, only if you allow it): a local reminder shortly before a free trial turns into a paid plan. It is scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore a subscription (section 6).
  • Camera: Printwell does not hold the camera permission. When you scan with the camera, the scan is captured by Google’s document scanner (part of Google Play services) or by your own camera app, and only the finished pages are handed back to Printwell.
  • Photos and files: you pick them with the Android photo picker or file picker, or share them to Printwell from another app. Printwell receives only the items you pick and cannot browse your library or storage.

3. Printing, scanning, text recognition and web pages

Print jobs go directly from your device to the printer you choose over your local network. Scans come directly from your printer’s scanner to your device. Your printer may keep its own job log, as printers do; that is outside our control.

Text recognition (OCR) and camera document scanning run on your device with Google ML Kit. Your images and recognised text are not sent to Google. Recognition models for some scripts (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Devanagari) may be downloaded by Google Play services the first time you need them. As described in Google’s ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information (such as device model, Android version, app package and version, and performance metrics) to maintain and improve the service; this is not linked to your identity and does not include your content.

When you print a web page, Printwell loads that page from the internet as a browser would, so the website receives a normal request from your device. Templates are generated on your device; no template content is sent anywhere.

4. Data stored on your device

What you choose to keep stays in the app’s private storage on your device: saved scans and their recognised text, recent print jobs, the printers you added and your preferences. Other apps cannot read it. Printwell is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this data never leaves the device through a backup. You can delete items in the app; uninstalling Printwell or clearing its storage removes all of its data.

5. Sharing and exports

When you share or export a PDF, a scan or recognised text, the Android share sheet or file picker sends it only to the destination you choose. From then on, that recipient’s or service’s privacy terms apply. Nothing is shared automatically.

6. Subscriptions and Google Play

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing address. The app asks Google Play on your device whether your subscription is active. Google provides us with transaction records needed to manage subscriptions and refunds (for example an order number, the plan, price, date and country). Google’s handling of your purchase is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Crash reports and ratings

If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may give us anonymised crash and performance reports for Printwell through Google Play Console. We use them only to fix bugs. The app may show Google Play’s in-app rating dialog; ratings and reviews are handled by Google, and we do not learn whether you were shown the dialog or what you chose.

8. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which may store messages on servers outside your country. We delete support conversations when they are no longer needed, or sooner if you ask. The legal basis is your request and our legitimate interest in answering it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).

9. Children

Printwell is a general-audience utility. It includes printable worksheets and coloring pages that parents and teachers can print for children, but it is not directed at children and has no account, chat, advertising or social features. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal data by email, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent. Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Article 11 gives you equivalent rights.

Because Printwell keeps your data only on your device, you exercise most of these rights directly: delete items in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything. For support emails, write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, for example your EU member state’s supervisory authority or, in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and we do not use it for targeted advertising.
